What we store

Account Your email address and password, held by Supabase Auth. The password is hashed and is never readable by this site.
Profile Your name, role (brand or event planner), organization, location, category and description. Stored in the public.users table.
Listings Events and opportunities you publish, including name, date, location, capacity, budget and description.
Connections Which opportunities you expressed interest in, and whether the other side accepted.
Messages Messages you send to, and receive from, the brands and planners you are connected with. A thread only exists on an accepted connection, and only the two people on that connection can read it. You can delete a message you sent.
Account deletion You can permanently delete your account from your profile page. Doing this removes your login, profile, events, opportunities, connections, and all messages you sent. There is no backup or recovery. The deletion is permanent and immediate.
Tracking None. There is no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking script on this site.

Who can see what

Row Level Security is enabled on every table, and the rules are in supabase-schema.sql. In plain terms:

  • Signed out: nothing. Every table rejects anonymous reads.
  • Signed in: all profiles, events and opportunities. The public users table holds no email, phone or other private field — your email lives only in Supabase Auth.
  • Your own rows: only you can edit your profile, and only the organizer can edit or delete their own events and opportunities.
  • Connections: only the two parties involved can see a connection.
  • Messages: only the two parties on that connection can read the thread, and a message can only be sent once the connection is accepted. A sender cannot edit a message after sending it.

You can edit or delete your profile data at any time from your profile page.

Terms of use

LinkUp connects brands with event planners. By using it you agree to:

  • Only publish events and opportunities that are real.
  • Do not impersonate another brand or organisation.
  • Do not use the site to send spam or unsolicited bulk messages.
  • Accept that listings and profiles are visible to other signed-in members.

The service is provided as-is, with no guarantee of uptime. Because it is a portfolio project with a free-tier database, it may be reset or taken down without notice.
